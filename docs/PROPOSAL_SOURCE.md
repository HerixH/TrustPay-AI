TrustPay AI

AI-Powered Smart Escrow & Fraud Detection for P2P Payments

**Problem**

P2P payments across Africa suffer from scams, lack of trust, and no protection for buyers and sellers. Freelancers get ghosted, buyers lose money, and there is no simple trust layer for transactions.

**Solution**

TrustPay AI introduces a smart escrow system combined with AI fraud detection. Funds are locked securely while AI analyzes conversations and behavior to detect scam risks before money is released.

**Core Features**

1. Smart Escrow (Blockchain-based)  
2. AI Fraud Detection (chat + behavior analysis)  
3. Risk Scoring Dashboard  
4. Voice Contract Confirmation  
5. Dispute Resolution System  

**How It Works**

1. Buyer creates a deal  
2. Funds are locked in escrow  
3. Buyer and seller communicate in-app  
4. AI analyzes messages and flags risks  
5. Payment is released or disputed  

Funds move on Solana; AI scoring runs off-chain on a Rust backend API.

**Tech Stack**

Mobile: React Native (Android & iOS). Backend: Rust (async HTTP API). Blockchain: Solana (devnet for demo; escrow lock/release on-chain). AI: OpenAI or similar via server-side NLP for risk scoring. Voice: ElevenLabs for voice contract confirmation.

**Why This Wins**

• Solves a real, urgent problem • Strong emotional and demo impact • Combines AI + Web3 meaningfully • Easy to understand for judges • Scalable into a real startup

**Demo Flow**

1. User initiates a deal  
2. Seller sends suspicious message  
3. AI flags scam risk  
4. Funds remain protected  
5. Legit transaction completes successfully  

**Conclusion**

TrustPay AI is not just a product — it is a trust infrastructure for digital payments, especially in emerging markets. It combines security, intelligence, and usability to solve one of the biggest problems in online transactions.
