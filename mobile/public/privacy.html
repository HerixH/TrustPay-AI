Privacy policy — TrustPay AI

Chain Minds · Last updated: 2026-05-09

This policy describes how the TrustPay AI application and its companion backend (“Services”) handle information. TrustPay AI is experimental software for demos and submissions; it is not offered as a regulated financial service.

What we collect and store

  • Deal records: On-chain escrow uses Solana wallet addresses you choose when creating or joining a deal (buyer, seller, arbiter), plus amounts and program identifiers our backend stores to coordinate the experience.
  • Chat messages: Message content you send through the app is stored on our API server (SQLite) with sender identifiers consistent with how you use the app.
  • Risk analysis results: When you run “Analyze,” our server scores conversation context and may store summaries or structured signals linked to the deal.
  • Wallet linking (mobile): Wallet adapter flows handle signing on your device; our backend receives addresses only when your usage sends them (for example as buyer/seller/arbiter strings).

Optional third-party processing

  • If configured by the operator, OpenAI may receive conversation text solely to enrich risk scoring.
  • If configured, ElevenLabs may receive script text to generate optional voice audio.
  • The app interacts with Solana RPC endpoints you or we configure; those providers see typical blockchain queries.

Security

Transport uses HTTPS where deployed correctly. The TrustPay API is designed so your signing keys remain with your wallet; our backend does not hold Solana private keys.

Retention

Data remains while the hosted SQLite database persists (typically until cleared by redeploy or operator maintenance). We do not promise indefinite retention or deletion tooling in demo deployments.

Children

The Services are not directed at children under 13.

Contact

For privacy questions about this deployment, contact the team operating this instance (Chain Minds / submission maintainer) through channels listed on your app listing or repository.

This document is provided for transparency in hackathon and demo submissions. It is not legal advice; consult counsel before production launch.
